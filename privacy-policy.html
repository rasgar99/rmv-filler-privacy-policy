Privacy Policy

RMV Form Filler  ·  Last updated: 26 July 2026

In short: RMV Form Filler does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on any server. Everything you type stays on your own device. There is no backend, no database, and no analytics.

What information the app handles

To fill in RMV vehicle transfer forms (M.T.A. 6, M.T.A. 8, and M.T.A. 3), the app asks you to enter:

  • Your finance company's name, address, and administrative details (saved once, reused for every form)
  • Customer / book owner details: name, address, NIC number
  • Vehicle registration details

Where this information is stored

All information you enter is stored only on your own device, using your device's local storage. It is never sent to, or stored on, any server operated by the app developer or any third party. The app has no internet-connected backend of its own — it does not need one, since all form-filling happens entirely on your device.

Generated PDF files

When you generate a filled form, the PDF is created entirely on your device and saved or shared using Android's own file system and share features (for example, to Google Drive, WhatsApp, or your device's Files app) — based on where you choose to send it. The app itself does not upload, transmit, or retain a copy of any generated PDF.

Third-party links (DMT eServices)

Starting with version 3.0, the app's home screen offers a shortcut to check vehicle details on the official Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) eServices portal (eservices.motortraffic.gov.lk). Tapping this opens the DMT website in an in-app browser tab (Chrome Custom Tabs), using your device's existing Chrome browser session.

The DMT eServices portal is owned and operated independently by the Department of Motor Traffic and is not affiliated with, or controlled by, RMV Form Filler. We do not collect, view, store, or transmit any information you enter on the DMT website — including your Google login, vehicle numbers, or search results. Because this feature reuses your device's browser session, if you're already signed in to a Google account in Chrome, that session may be used automatically when the DMT portal opens.

Please refer to the Department of Motor Traffic's own privacy policy and terms for information on how they collect and handle your data on their portal.

Subscription payments

If you choose to subscribe, payment is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app never sees, collects, or stores your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — that information is handled directly by Google, under Google's own Privacy Policy.

No advertising, no tracking, no analytics

This app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics libraries, or any other third-party tracking tools. We do not know how you use the app, and we have no way of finding out.

Deleting your data

Since all data lives only on your device, you can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → RMV Form Filler → Storage → Clear Data) or by uninstalling the app entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
