Delete Your Account

RMV Form Filler  ·  Last updated: 4 September 2026

In short: Email us and we'll delete your account, sign-in record, subscription-status record, and generation count within 7 days. This does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription — that's managed separately through your Google Play account.

How to request deletion

Step 1. Send an email to srgdigital.dev@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my RMV Form Filler account."
Step 2. Include the Google account email address you used to sign in to RMV Form Filler (the Android app or the web portal at www.rmvfiller.app).
Step 3. We'll confirm your identity matches the account on file, then delete your data within 7 days, and reply to confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in record (email address, display name, account identifier)
  • Your subscription-status record (whether you hold an active Pro subscription, and its expiry)
  • Your free-generation count

What is not affected

  • An active Google Play subscription is not cancelled by this process. If you're subscribed to any tier (Leasing, Individual, or Pro), you'll continue to be billed until you cancel it separately through Google Play: Play Store app → Menu → Subscriptions.
  • Finance Company details saved locally on your device or browser are not part of this deletion request, since we never receive or store them ourselves. Clear these yourself via Android Settings → Apps → RMV Form Filler → Storage → Clear Data, or your browser's site data for www.rmvfiller.app.

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at the same address: srgdigital.dev@gmail.com
